## Important limitations

GeoRisk AI is a demonstration application.

Its GIS screening results and Stångån scenarios
are indicative and are not official flood-risk
assessments or verified flood-inundation models.

The results must not be used as the sole basis
for safety-critical or property-planning decisions.
